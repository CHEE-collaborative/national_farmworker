# Heat and wildfire smoke exposures among farmworkers in the U.S.
**Authors**: Jamie Ponmattam<sup>1</sup> and Daniel Carrión*<sup>2,3</sup>

**Abstract**: US farmworkers face rising co-exposure to extreme heat and wildfire smoke without federal occupational protection. Using county-level heat index, wildfire smoke, and farmworker counts from 2008–2023, we show that emerging hotspots of co-exposure, and the steepest increases for migrant farmworkers, concentrate in states without heat or smoke standards. These climate-driven occupational hazards are increasingly concentrated in jurisdictions without worker protection.
